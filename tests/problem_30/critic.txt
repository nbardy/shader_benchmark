**Inputs**

1. *Spec* – the exact text of the barbell shape generation prompt
2. *Candidate image* – the shader output produced from that spec

**Task**
Assess the candidate image's adherence to the barbell visualization specification. Provide a score **S** on the scale 1-10 using the rubric below. Include a concise justification (≤ 150 words).

**Rubric**

| Aspect | Points | Guide |
|--------|--------|--------|
| **Shape Accuracy** | 0-3 | Two spheres of correct size connected by cylinder? Proper positioning and proportions? Smooth blending at junctions? |
| **Material Quality** | 0-2 | Metallic PBR materials correctly applied? Distinct finishes for spheres (chrome) vs cylinder (brushed)? |
| **Lighting Setup** | 0-2 | Three-light configuration implemented? Correct intensities and positions? Soft shadows visible? |
| **Rotation and Camera** | 0-1 | Initial 15° tilt visible? Smooth rotation animation? Camera angle shows shape clearly? |
| **Rendering Quality** | 0-2 | 1600×1600 resolution achieved? 8× antialiasing smooth? Background gradient correct? |

**Output Format**

```text
Score: S/10
Rationale: <brief explanation of scoring decisions>
```

**Scoring Guidelines**
- Perfect implementation scores 10
- Incorrect shape or missing components caps score at ≤ 3
- Poor material differentiation reduces score by 1-2 points
- Missing or incorrect lighting reduces score by 1-2 points
- Low quality rendering or aliasing reduces score by 1 point
