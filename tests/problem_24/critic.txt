Evaluate the submission for the Star Tetrahedron (Stella Octangula) visualization.

The stella octangula is a compound of two tetrahedra that creates an eight-pointed star when their vertices extend outward. The intersection of the two tetrahedra forms a regular octahedron at the center. This classical geometric construction demonstrates beautiful symmetry properties.

Score based on geometric accuracy and visual clarity of the compound structure.
