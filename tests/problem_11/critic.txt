Evaluate the submission for the Alexander-Polynomial "Petal Radar" visualization.

Key evaluation criteria:
1. Correct six-petal symmetry with peaks at proper angles
2. Accurate peak radius (√2 ≈ 1.414)
3. Proper polar grid with concentric circles and radial spokes
4. Visual quality of the hot-pink neon glow effect

The Alexander polynomial of the trefoil knot, when evaluated on the unit circle, creates a beautiful six-petal pattern. This visualization should clearly show the polynomial's magnitude as a function of angle, forming a symmetric flower-like shape that demonstrates important topological invariants of the trefoil knot.

Score based on mathematical accuracy and visual presentation quality.

Ground-truth key numbers:
- Peak radius r_max = √2 ≈ 1.41421356
- Petal angle spacing Δθ = 60° between successive maxima/minima crossings

Evaluation recipe:
1. Polar curve reconstruction – scan image; convert pink pixels using inverse polar transform to (θ,r)
2. Petal-count & symmetry (4 pts)
   - Detect local maxima; #peaks=6 ±0
   - Angular deviation from multiples of 60° ≤ 1°
3. Radius accuracy (2 pts)
   - Mean observed r_max̂ over 6 peaks; error ≤ 2% → full, ≤ 4% → 1 pt
4. Grid presence (1 pt) – at least five concentric and 12 spokes
5. Stroke/Glow integrity (2 pts)
   - Core stroke width 6 ± 0.6 px (+1)
   - Glow band FWHM 6 ± 1 px (+1)
6. Colour fidelity (1 pt) – ΔE*94 between stroke and #FF0088 < 5
