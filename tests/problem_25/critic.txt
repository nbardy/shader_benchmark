Evaluate the submission for the Perfect Tetrahedron visualization.

Key evaluation criteria:
1. Regular tetrahedron with all edges equal length
2. Correct angular relationships between faces
3. Proper circumsphere radius normalization
4. Golden material rendering with appropriate lighting

The regular tetrahedron is the simplest Platonic solid with four equilateral triangular faces. It represents perfect three-dimensional symmetry with the minimum number of faces possible for a polyhedron.

Score based on geometric perfection and rendering quality.

- All 6 edges equal length within 2% (5 pts)
  - Edge equality: all edges must be equal

  - Face normals angles: arccos(1/3) ≈ 70.53°
- Circumscribed sphere radius 1 ± 2% (2 pts)
  - Circumsphere radius: √(3/8) when edge length = 1
