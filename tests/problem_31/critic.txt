**Inputs**

1. *Spec* – the exact text of the torus visualization prompt
2. *Candidate image* – the shader output produced from that spec

**Task**
Evaluate the candidate image's implementation of the mathematical torus with advanced rendering features. Provide a score **S** on the scale 1-10 using the rubric below. Include a focused justification (≤ 150 words).

**Rubric**

| Aspect | Points | Guide |
|--------|--------|--------|
| **Mathematical Form** | 0-3 | Correct torus shape with R=2.0, r=0.7? Smooth surface with proper parametric construction? Oscillating minor radius visible? |
| **Color and Curvature** | 0-2 | HSV coloring based on (u,v) parameters? Gaussian curvature visualization with red/blue tints? |
| **Surface Details** | 0-2 | Parametric grid lines visible and properly spaced? Glossy ceramic material appearance? |
| **Animation Quality** | 0-1 | Dual rotation (y-axis and major axis) implemented? Smooth minor radius oscillation? |
| **Rendering Excellence** | 0-2 | Three-point lighting setup? Ambient occlusion? DOF blur? 1600×1600 with antialiasing? |

**Output Format**

```text
Score: S/10
Rationale: <targeted explanation of scoring>
```

**Scoring Guidelines**
- Perfect execution scores 10
- Incorrect torus proportions or missing oscillation caps at ≤ 5
- Missing curvature visualization reduces by 1-2 points
- Absent grid lines or poor material reduces by 1-2 points
- Technical deficiencies (resolution, aliasing) reduce by 1 point
