Evaluate the submission for the Geometric Cube visualization.

Key evaluation criteria:
1. Correct wireframe representation with 12 edges
2. Proper hidden-line rendering with dashed lines
3. Orthographic projection maintaining parallel edges
4. Appropriate coloring and transparency

The cube is the most fundamental 3D shape, representing perfect regularity in three dimensions. The wireframe visualization with hidden-line rendering is a classic computer graphics challenge that tests understanding of 3D projection and visibility.

Score based on geometric accuracy and proper rendering techniques.

---

Tests:
1. Edge count 12; hidden dashed edges 7 visible (+3 pts)
   - Edge count: exactly 12 edges
   - Hidden edges rendered as dashed lines
2. All visible edges equal pixel length ±1% (+3 pts)
   - Parallel edges: must remain parallel in orthographic projection
3. Orthographic parallel edges perfectly parallel (angular dev <0.1°) (+2 pts)
   - Orthographic projection: no perspective distortion
4. Colour and dash pattern correct (+2 pts)
   - Color: proper distinction between visible and hidden edges
   - Dash pattern: consistent for all hidden edges

Score: S/10
