Evaluate the submission for the Fourier-Blueprint "Frequency → Architecture" visualization.

Key evaluation criteria:

This visualization demonstrates the Fourier transform relationship between frequency and spatial domains in an architectural metaphor. Low frequencies (near the origin in frequency space) should create tall walls around the perimeter of the floorplan, while high frequencies create fine interior details, illustrating how different frequencies contribute to spatial structure.

Score based on mathematical accuracy of the Fourier transform and the clarity of the architectural representation.
