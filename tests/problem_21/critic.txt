Evaluate the submission for the 3D Fractal Tree visualization.

Key evaluation criteria:
1. Correct binary tree topology with depth 7 and 127 segments
2. Accurate branching angles (45° from parent, ±35° rotation)
3. Proper length scaling (factor 0.7 per level)
4. Appropriate radius tapering (factor 0.6 per level)
5. Visual quality with realistic bark texture and lighting

The 3D fractal tree should appear organic and natural despite its mathematical construction. The recursive branching pattern creates a convincing winter tree silhouette, with smaller branches becoming progressively thinner and shorter according to the scaling rules.

Score based on mathematical accuracy of the recursive structure and visual realism of the tree rendering.

---

Expectations:
- Segment count N=127
- Branch length of level k = 0.7^k

Checks:
1. Topology (3 pts) – BFS through centreline graph must yield binary tree depth = 7 and N = 127 ± 0
2. Angle accuracy (2 pts) – for 200 random branch splits, mean branch angle 45° ± 3°
3. Length scaling (2 pts) – least-squares fit of len vs depth slope log0.7 ± 0.05
4. Radius scaling (1 pt) – end-radius level 7 ≈ 0.08·0.6^7
5. Render clarity (2 pts) – background gradient exact, no aliasing on twig tips

Output format:
Score: S/10
