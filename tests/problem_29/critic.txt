**Inputs**

1. *Spec* – the exact text of the icosahedron generation prompt
2. *Candidate image* – the shader output produced from that spec

**Task**
Evaluate how accurately the candidate image implements the specified regular icosahedron visualization. Provide a score **S** on the scale 1-10 using the rubric below. Include a brief justification (≤ 150 words).

**Rubric**

| Aspect | Points | Guide |
|--------|--------|--------|
| **Geometric Accuracy** | 0-3 | Correct icosahedral structure with 20 triangular faces, 12 vertices, 30 edges? Proper vertex placement on unit sphere? |
| **Face Coloring** | 0-2 | Gradient coloring based on face normals as specified? Smooth HSV mapping with correct hue/saturation/value? |
| **Edge and Vertex Rendering** | 0-2 | Dark grey edges visible and properly connected? White vertex spheres at all 12 positions? |
| **Lighting and Shading** | 0-1 | Phong shading implemented with specified parameters? Light positioned correctly? |
| **Technical Quality** | 0-2 | Proper resolution (1600×1600), antialiasing, background gradient, smooth rotation? |

**Output Format**

```text
Score: S/10
Rationale: <concise explanation of scoring>
```

**Scoring Guidelines**
- Perfect implementation scores 10
- Missing or incorrect vertex/face structure caps score at ≤ 4
- Incorrect coloring scheme reduces score by 1-2 points
- Missing edges or vertices reduces score by 1-2 points
- Poor antialiasing or resolution issues reduce score by 1 point
