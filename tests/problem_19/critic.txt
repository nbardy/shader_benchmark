The Sierpinski tetrahedron is a 3D fractal that extends the concept of the Sierpinski triangle into three dimensions. The visualization should clearly show the self-similar voids that appear at each level of recursion, creating a crystalline structure with intricate geometric patterns.

Score based on mathematical accuracy of the fractal construction and visual quality of the icy quartz appearance.
