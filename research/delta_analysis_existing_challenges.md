## Identified Gaps

### 1. Mathematical Domains Under-represented:
- **Algebraic Geometry**: No challenges focusing on algebraic curves/surfaces
- **Computational Geometry**: Limited algorithmic geometry challenges
- **Complex Analysis**: Few complex function visualizations
- **Group Theory/Symmetry**: Limited symmetry group visualizations
- **Differential Equations**: Beyond physics simulations
- **Number Theory**: No visualizations of number-theoretic concepts
- **Graph Theory**: No network/graph visualizations
- **Parametric Surfaces**: Limited coverage beyond basic shapes

### 2. Difficulty Gaps:
- **Severe lack of beginner challenges** (only 4.7%)
- Need more progressive difficulty ramp
- Intermediate challenges could bridge better to advanced

### 3. Visual Art/Beauty Gaps:
- Limited mathematical art patterns
- Few generative/procedural patterns
- Limited color theory applications
- Few challenges focusing on aesthetic beauty

### 4. Technical Gaps:
- Compute shaders underutilized in main challenges
- Limited vertex shader specific challenges
- Few multi-pass rendering challenges
- Limited use of advanced GPU features

### 5. Application Domain Gaps:
- Architecture/Design patterns
- Biological/Natural patterns
- Musical/Sound visualization
- Data visualization techniques
- Abstract mathematical art
