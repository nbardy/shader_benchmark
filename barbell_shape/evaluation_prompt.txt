**Inputs**

1. *Spec* – the generation prompt for the Barbell Shape.
2. *Candidate* – the PNG produced by the renderer.

**Task** Return an integer **S** (1‑10) plus a ≤ 180‑word rationale.

**Expected geometry**
Sphere centres 4 apart, cylinder length 4, tangent continuity.

**Scoring**
0‑4 geometry, 0‑2 tangency, 0‑2 shading, 0‑2 render.

**Output format**

```
Score: S/10
Rationale: <text>
```
