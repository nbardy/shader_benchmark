**Inputs**

1. *Spec* – the generation prompt for the Five-Pointed Star Polygon.
2. *Candidate* – the PNG produced by the renderer.

**Task** Return an integer **S** (1‑10) plus a ≤ 180‑word rationale.

**Expectations**
Five identical isosceles points; central pentagon edge length = outer edge/τ. Outline continuous.

**Scoring**
0‑4 geometry (equal angles 36° & 108°), 0‑2 golden‑ratio internal ratios, 0‑2 outline, 0‑2 fill uniform.

**Output format**

```
Score: S/10
Rationale: <text>
```
