**Inputs**

1. *Spec* – the generation prompt for the Sierpinski Triangle.
2. *Candidate* – the PNG produced by the renderer.

**Task** Return an integer **S** (1‑10) plus a ≤ 180‑word rationale.

**Checks**
Total small triangles = $3^{6}=729$; black outline matches level‑0. Gaps exactly equilateral.

**Scoring**
0‑4 recursion depth, 0‑2 count, 0‑2 alignment, 0‑2 stroke quality.

**Output format**

```
Score: S/10
Rationale: <text>
```
