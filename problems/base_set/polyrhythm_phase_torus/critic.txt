__MATHEMATICAL_ACCURACY__
Winding Numbers:
- Does the trajectory wind 7 times around the torus major direction over one common cycle?
- Does it wind 5 times around the torus minor direction over one common cycle?
- Is the 11-phase visible as an additional vertical/color modulation rather than replacing the 5:7 torus winding?
- Is the trajectory closed after \(\tau=2\pi\)?
- Is the trajectory smooth and continuous without breaks?

Beat Alignments:
- Are all-beat alignments at the start/end marked as the largest markers?
- Are 5–7 pair alignments represented at three points including endpoints?
- Are 5–11 pair alignments represented at seven points including endpoints?
- Are 7–11 pair alignments represented at five points including endpoints?
- Are marker types visually distinguishable, with endpoints not overcounted confusingly?

Phase Encoding:
- Does the trajectory color cycle according to \(\theta_{11}\) and therefore repeat 11 times?
- Are reference torus grid lines consistent with 5 minor and 7 major guide cycles?
- Do the beat wheels have exactly 5, 7, and 11 tick marks?
- Is the phase-color strip, if present, consistent with the trajectory coloring?

__VISUAL_IMPLEMENTATION__
Torus and Trajectory Rendering:
- Is the transparent reference torus smooth and readable?
- Is the trajectory tube smooth, continuous, and not overly thick?
- Does the camera reveal the torus hole and path windings?
- Are grid lines thin enough to avoid visual clutter?

Marker Rendering:
- Are alignment markers visible and attached to the trajectory?
- Are all-beat markers visually dominant but not obscuring nearby pair markers?
- Are different pair-alignment types distinguishable by shape, ring, or color?
- Are markers anti-aliased and not hidden behind opaque surfaces?

Composition and Contrast:
- Is the color contrast high enough to distinguish trajectory from reference torus?
- Are beat wheels readable in the corner?
- Does the overall image avoid degenerating into an unreadable knot of color?
- Is lighting/shading sufficient to show 3D depth?

__COMPLETENESS_AND_SPECIFICATIONS__
Required Elements:
- Transparent reference torus is present.
- Main \(5{:}7{:}11\) trajectory tube is present.
- Trajectory hue varies by \(\theta_{11}\).
- All-beat and three pair-alignment marker types are present.
- Three beat wheels with 5, 7, and 11 ticks are present.
- Reference grid or guide rings are present.

Technical Requirements:
- Output is a single PNG at least 1600×1600 px.
- Static frame only.
- The visualization is self-contained and does not rely on sound playback.
- Camera angle supports counting or inferring windings.

Failure Modes to Penalize:
- Generic torus knot without 5:7:11 structure.
- Missing 11-phase color modulation.
- Missing or incorrectly counted beat wheels.
- No alignment markers.
- Opaque torus hiding the trajectory.
