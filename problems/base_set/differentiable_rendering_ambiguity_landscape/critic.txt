__MATHEMATICAL_ACCURACY__
Silhouette Ambiguity:
- Does the main valley follow the curve \(r=0.42z\) across the \((z,r)\) domain?
- Are the five candidate sphere glyphs placed along this curve with increasing radius and depth?
- Do the candidate glyphs communicate equal projected silhouette radius despite different physical scenes?
- Does the loss rise away from the ambiguity curve?
- Is there a mild preference/global low near \(z=3,r=1.26\) due to the small depth prior?

Loss Surface and Contours:
- Is the surface smooth and shaped like a long shallow valley rather than isolated unrelated basins?
- Are iso-loss contours elongated along the ambiguity direction?
- Is contour spacing tighter across the valley than along it?
- Does the target thumbnail correspond to a centered circular silhouette of apparent radius \(0.42\)?

Albedo/Light Inset:
- Does the secondary inset show a hyperbolic ambiguity \(a\ell=0.8\)?
- Does the inset loss increase away from the hyperbola?
- Are albedo and light intensity presented as separate axes or directions?
- Is the inset visually linked to the same inverse-problem ambiguity theme?

__VISUAL_IMPLEMENTATION__
Main Landscape Rendering:
- Is the height field smooth and adequately sampled?
- Are at least 14 contour levels visible and aligned with the surface height?
- Is the glowing valley line visible without obscuring the surface geometry?
- Does lighting make the valley and surrounding ridges readable?

Glyph and Thumbnail Rendering:
- Are the five sphere/camera glyphs clearly different in physical size/depth?
- Are equal-size projected circles or image-plane marks shown for the five glyphs?
- Is the target thumbnail simple, clean, and recognisable?
- Are camera rays thin and not visually confused with contour lines?

Composition:
- Does the main loss surface dominate the canvas?
- Are the target and albedo/light insets placed cleanly without blocking the main valley?
- Are colors used consistently for low/high loss?
- Is anti-aliasing adequate on contours, glyph rays, and inset curves?

- The ambiguity curve \(r=0.42z\) is explicitly highlighted.

Technical Requirements:

- Axis directions or tick cues are included for the main landscape.

Failure Modes to Penalize:
