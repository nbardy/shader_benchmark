__MATHEMATICAL_ACCURACY__
Double-Gyre Flow Structure:
- Does the basin show two gyre-like regions across the `x ∈ [0,2]` domain?
- Is the domain rectangular with aspect ratio approximately `2:1`?
- Are flow features consistent with a time-dependent double gyre rather than random noise?
- Do tracer paths curve around gyre lobes and separatrix-like structures?

LCS / FTLE Representation:
- Are warm repelling and cool attracting LCS ridges both present?
- Do LCS ridges appear as coherent material curves or ribbons, not scattered dots?
- Are ridges located near separatrices and lobe boundaries between gyres?
- If a height field is used, do elevated or bright ridges correspond to FTLE-like maxima?
- Are attracting and repelling structures visually distinct but allowed to intersect/transverse each other?

Trajectory Integration:
- Are at least dozens of tracer trajectories present?
- Do trajectories have fading tails or direction cues suggesting integration over time?
- Do stream/tracer curves respect basin boundaries and not escape arbitrarily?
- Are sparse velocity arrows, if present, aligned with the double-gyre pattern?

__VISUAL_IMPLEMENTATION__
LCS Rendering Quality:
- Are ridges crisp, continuous, and easy to distinguish from background texture?
- Are warm and cool colors used consistently for repelling and attracting structures?
- Is transparency handled well where ridges overlap?
- Are lines anti-aliased and not jagged?

Flow Field Readability:
- Is the ocean basin background subtle rather than overpowering?
- Are tracer paths thin enough to avoid clutter but visible enough to inspect?
- Does the image show both local curve detail and global two-gyre organization?
- If rendered obliquely, is FTLE height visible without hiding the basin layout?

Composition and Legend:
- Is there a clear legend or equivalent color key?
- Are domain ticks or boundary markings visible?
- Does the camera or top-down framing preserve the `2:1` basin geometry?
- Is the white page/background clean and professional?

__COMPLETENESS_AND_SPECIFICATIONS__
Required Elements:
- Rectangular double-gyre basin is present.
- Warm repelling LCS ridges are present.
- Cool attracting LCS ridges are present.
- Tracer trajectories are present.
- Legend or color key is present.

Technical Requirements:
- Image resolution is at least `1600 × 1600` pixels.
- Output is a single static PNG.
- Static frame corresponds to `t0 = 0` analysis, not an animation.
- Main basin is fully visible and not clipped.

Benchmark-Specific Failure Modes:
- If the image is a generic ocean map with no LCS ridges, mathematical accuracy should be low.
- If only streamlines are shown and attracting/repelling ridges are omitted, completeness should be penalized.
- If the domain is not recognizably double-gyre-like, mathematical accuracy should be penalized.
- If warm/cool roles are swapped or undefined, visual implementation and completeness should be penalized.
