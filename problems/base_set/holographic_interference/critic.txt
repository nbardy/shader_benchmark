**Interference pattern spacing (0-3 pts)**

**Color superposition accuracy (0-2 pts)**

**Contrast and intensity range (0-2 pts)**

- Minimum intensity < 5% in every channel
- Maximum intensity > 95% in every channel
- No moiré patterns or aliasing artifacts

**Frame and boundary quality (0-1 pts)**

- Exterior must be pure black (#000000)
