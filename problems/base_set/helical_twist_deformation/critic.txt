**Inputs**

1. *Spec* – the generation prompt for Helical Twist Deformation.
2. *Candidate* – the PNG produced by the renderer.

**Task** Return an integer **S** (1‑10) plus a ≤ 180‑word rationale.

## __MATHEMATICAL_ACCURACY__

**Helical twist linearity (0-3 pts)**
- Measure rotation angle at 10 heights across the cube
- Rotation should vary linearly with height (R² > 0.99)
- Full 360° rotation from bottom to top
- Verify consistent angular velocity throughout twist

**Geometric deformation quality (0-2 pts)**
- No discontinuities in surface parametrization
- Absence of self-intersections or topology errors
- Beveled edges maintain consistent radius throughout
- Proper preservation of cube volume during deformation

## __VISUAL_IMPLEMENTATION__

**Material and reflection rendering (0-2 pts)**
- Metallic surface with physically accurate Fresnel reflections
- Reflections must be visible at grazing angles
- Proper index of refraction for metallic materials
- Surface roughness consistent with specification

**Color gradient implementation (0-2 pts)**
- Smooth transition from blue at bottom to golden yellow at top
- Hue variation must be < ±15° from expected values
- No banding or discontinuities in gradient
- Color space accuracy and gamma correction

**Lighting and shadow quality (0-1 pts)**
- Three distinct light sources clearly visible
- Soft shadows properly rendered and positioned
- Consistent illumination across twisted surface
- No harsh lighting artifacts or over-exposure
