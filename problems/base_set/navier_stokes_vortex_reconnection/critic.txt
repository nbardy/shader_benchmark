__MATHEMATICAL_ACCURACY__
Reconnection Topology:
- Are exactly two main post-reconnection vortex tubes visible?
- Does one tube connect upper-left to lower-right and the other lower-left to upper-right?
- Are there two distinct bridge arcs rather than a single X-shaped crossing?
- Do the bridges remain separated in depth, avoiding an impossible physical intersection?
- Are four narrowed neck/collar regions visible near the bridge endpoints?

Centerline and Orientation Consistency:
- Do the outer arms extend to approximately \(x=\pm L\) with offsets \(y=\pm d\)?
- Are tangent arrows aligned along the tubes rather than randomly placed?
- Does the helicity/tangent color change consistently with tangent direction?
- Are the ghost filaments anti-parallel and placed at \(y=\pm d,z=0\)?

Fluid-Visualization Semantics:
- Does the vorticity cloud concentrate near the reconnection/bridge region?
- Does the \(z=0\) cutting plane show stronger magnitude near the center than far away?
- Is the image recognisable as vortex reconnection rather than generic intertwined tubes?
- Is the topology visually inspectable despite volume effects?

__VISUAL_IMPLEMENTATION__
Tube Quality:
- Are tubes smooth, round, and continuous over all centerline pieces?
- Are there no gaps, sharp unintended kinks, or self-intersection artifacts at bridge joins?
- Does the radius narrowing look controlled and localised near the four necks?
- Are tube surfaces shaded enough to reveal 3D curvature?

Volumetric and Plane Rendering:
- Is the vorticity cloud translucent and spatially aligned with the tubes?
- Is the cutting plane semi-transparent, readable, and not confused with a solid wall?
- Are dashed ghost filaments visibly thinner and secondary to the main tubes?
- Does the central glow enhance, rather than obscure, the reconnection.

Camera and Readability:
- Does the perspective reveal that the two bridges pass at different depths?
- Are all four outer arms visible within the frame?
- Are tangent arrows visible but not so dense that they clutter the topology?
- Is anti-aliasing adequate on tubes, arrows, and dashed curves?

__COMPLETENESS_AND_SPECIFICATIONS__
Required Elements:
- Two main reconnected vortex tubes are present.
- Two grey dashed pre-reconnection ghost filaments are present.
- Tangent arrows are present along the tubes.
- Vorticity cloud around the bridge region is present.
- A semi-transparent \(z=0\) cutting plane is present.
- Four pinch collars or neck indicators are visible.

Technical Requirements:
- Output is a single PNG at least 1600×1600 px.
- The image is a static frame at the specified reconnection stage.
- The orientation triad or equivalent spatial cue is included.
- Tube topology remains readable at full-image scale.

Failure Modes to Penalize:
- Merely drawing two crossing cylinders with no reconnection bridges.
- Missing dashed pre-reconnection reference filaments.
- Tubes that visually intersect without depth separation.
- Overly opaque volume rendering that hides the topology.
- Random decorative arrows not aligned with tangent flow.
