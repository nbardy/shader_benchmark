__MATHEMATICAL_ACCURACY__
Fold Geometry:
- Does the tissue sheet follow the specified folded height profile with a raised hinge near \(y=-0.18\)?
- Is the secondary depression near \(y=0.45\) visible?
- Does the fold vary across \(x\) through the \((1-0.15x^2)\) term rather than forming a perfectly uniform ridge?
- Is the cross-section inset consistent with the main 3D fold profile?

Cellular-Potts Semantics:
- Are individual cells visible as a hexagonal/staggered epithelial tiling?
- Are hinge-region cells apically constricted, i.e. smaller/narrower than surrounding cells?
- Are boundaries near the hinge or clone interfaces darker/thicker, reflecting higher tension?
- Are the four lineage clone regions seeded in approximately the specified quadrants?
- Are exactly or approximately 8 T1-like neighbour-exchange events highlighted along the hinge?

Force and Topology Cues:
- Do force arrows point inward toward the hinge band?
- Are T1 markers localised to cell boundaries/diamonds rather than arbitrary red dots?
- Does the tissue remain a continuous sheet, not disconnected patches?
- Does the visualization read as a cellular tissue fold rather than a generic colored surface?

__VISUAL_IMPLEMENTATION__
Cell Rendering:
- Are cell polygons individually visible and conforming to the folded surface?
- Are boundaries crisp but not so thick that they hide cell colors?
- Are clone colors distinguishable and visually coherent across neighbouring cells?
- Are hinge cells visibly deformed/constricted without causing severe overlap artifacts?

Fold and Lighting:
- Does the oblique camera reveal 3D height and surface curvature?
- Is shading sufficient to perceive the ridge and depression?
- Are force arrows and T1 glyphs visible above the cell layer?
- Is anti-aliasing adequate on many small cell boundaries?

Inset and Legend:
- Is the side cross-section inset readable and correctly placed?
- Is the hinge marked in the inset and on the main sheet?
- Is the lineage legend compact and consistent with cell colors?
- Is the T1-event marker explained visually by the legend or repeated glyph style?

__COMPLETENESS_AND_SPECIFICATIONS__
Required Elements:
- Approximately \(34\times28\) staggered/hexagonal cells are present.
- Folded 3D sheet with hinge near \(y=-0.18\) is present.
- Apical constriction near the hinge is visible.
- Four lineage clone color regions are present.
- Dark cell boundaries and increased hinge/interface tension are visible.
- Eight T1-like event markers are present along the hinge.
- Inward force arrows are present.
- Cross-section inset and lineage/T1 legend are present.

Technical Requirements:
- Output is a single PNG at least 1600×1600 px.
- Static frame only.
- Background is white or very light grey.
- The image is self-contained and does not rely on external data.

Failure Modes to Penalize:
- Smooth colored sheet with no individual cells.
- No apical constriction at the hinge.
- Clone colors placed randomly rather than as four coherent seeded regions.
- Missing T1-event markers.
- Missing cross-section inset or force arrows.
