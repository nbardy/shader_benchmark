__MATHEMATICAL_ACCURACY__
Fault Geometry:
- Is a rectangular dipping fault plane visible beneath or intersecting a surface plane?
- Does the fault dip at an oblique angle around `35°`, not vertical or horizontal?
- Is the fault elongated along strike and shorter down dip, roughly matching a `60 km × 20 km` rectangle?
- Is a surface trace of the dipping fault visible on or near the `Z = 0` plane?

Rupture and Slip Field:
- Is the hypocenter visible on the fault near one side rather than at the exact center?
- Is the rupture front drawn as a clipped curve on the fault plane around the hypocenter?
- Does the slip heatmap peak near the rupture-front band / hypocentral region and fade elsewhere?
- Are displacement vectors located on the fault and scaled by slip amplitude?
- Does the heatmap use blue/low through red/high or another clear monotone slip scale?

Wavefront Geometry:
- Are two wavefront shells centered at the hypocenter?
- Is the P-wave shell larger than the S-wave shell, approximately radius ratio `48:28` or `~1.7:1`?
- Is the P shell blue and the S shell orange, or otherwise clearly distinguished?
- Do shells intersect or surround the fault volume consistently with the hypocenter location?

__VISUAL_IMPLEMENTATION__
3D Scene Readability:
- Does the camera angle reveal the surface plane, the dipping fault, and shells simultaneously?
- Are wavefront shells translucent enough to see the fault and slip through them?
- Are shadows/depth cues sufficient to locate the hypocenter and rupture front in 3D?
- Is the fault plane not clipped or hidden by the shells?

Slip and Vector Rendering:
- Is the slip colorbar or swatch readable?
- Are displacement vectors short, aligned, and not so dense that they obscure the heatmap?
- Is the rupture front bright and visually distinct from displacement vectors?
- Are the heatmap gradients smooth rather than blocky or random?

Surface and Scale Elements:
- Is the surface grid subtle and helpful for orientation?
- Is a scale bar or ticked equivalent present?
- Are P/S shell labels or color distinctions clear?
- Are antialiasing and line quality sufficient for the fault trace and rupture curve?

__COMPLETENESS_AND_SPECIFICATIONS__
Required Elements:
- Dipping rectangular fault plane is present.
- Slip heatmap is present.
- Rupture front curve is present.
- Hypocenter marker is present.
- P and S wavefront shells are both present.
- Displacement vectors and surface plane are present.

Technical Requirements:
- Image resolution is at least `1600 × 1600` pixels.
- Output is a single static PNG.
- Background is white or very light grey.
- Main scene is fully in frame and not excessively occluded.

Benchmark-Specific Failure Modes:
- If wavefront shells are not centered on the hypocenter, mathematical accuracy should be penalized.
- If the P and S radii are reversed, mathematical accuracy should be penalized.
- If the fault is not dipping or not rectangular, mathematical accuracy should be low.
- If the output is a generic earthquake icon/map without 3D fault geometry, completeness should be low.
