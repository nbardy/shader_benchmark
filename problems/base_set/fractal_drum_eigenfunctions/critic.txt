__MATHEMATICAL_ACCURACY__
Fractal Domain:
- Is the visible domain an order-5 or clearly high-order Sierpiński carpet with recursively removed central squares?
- Are holes actually absent/punched through rather than merely painted a different color?
- Are multiple scales of holes visible, including large central holes and smaller recursive holes?
- Is the outer boundary square and the recursive grid alignment consistent with ternary subdivision?

Eigenfunction Field:
- Does the field vanish on the outer boundary and inside removed holes?
- Are positive and negative domains both present and spatially interleaved?
- Is the dominant antisymmetry across the main diagonal visible?
- Do additional higher-frequency terms create secondary nodal branches or loops rather than a single trivial line?
- Is plate height consistent with signed field value, not just color?

Nodal Structure:
- Is the zero contour explicitly drawn as dark nodal curves?
- Do nodal curves lie on color-transition boundaries between positive and negative regions?
- Are nodal lines clipped correctly by holes and boundaries?
- Do nodal domains respect the fractal holes instead of crossing removed regions?

__VISUAL_IMPLEMENTATION__
Fractal Plate Rendering:
- Are hole outlines crisp across multiple recursive scales?
- Is the plate thin but visibly three-dimensional?
- Are shadows and lighting sufficient to distinguish holes from merely dark painted squares?
- Is the height deformation smooth over retained regions?

Color and Contour Rendering:
- Is the positive/negative diverging color map clear and symmetric?
- Are near-zero regions pale, with dark nodal curves readable on top?
- Are boundary outlines and nodal lines not confused with one another?
- Is anti-aliasing adequate on tiny fractal holes?

Layout and Side Strip:
- Is the oblique top-down camera angle suitable for both height and domain recognition?
- Is the spectral side strip visible and distinct from the main plate?
- Are six eigenvalue bars present with the fifth bar highlighted?
- Is the image uncluttered despite high fractal detail?

__COMPLETENESS_AND_SPECIFICATIONS__
Required Elements:
- Order-5 Sierpiński carpet domain is present.
- Signed elevated eigenfunction surface is present.
- Punched holes at multiple recursive scales are present.
- Explicit dark nodal curves are present.
- Positive/zero/negative color encoding is present.
- Spectral side strip with six bars and highlighted fifth mode is present.

Technical Requirements:
- Output is a single PNG at least 1600×1600 px.
- Background is white or very light grey.
- The visualization is static and self-contained.
- The main plate occupies most of the canvas.

Failure Modes to Penalize:
- Drawing a solid square heatmap without punched holes.
- Showing a generic fractal texture unrelated to the Sierpiński carpet.
- Missing nodal curves or mismatched nodal curves that do not follow color sign changes.
- No visible signed height deformation.
- Missing spectral side strip.
