__MATHEMATICAL_ACCURACY__
Function and Branch Points:
- Are the four branch points located at approximately \(\pm1\) and \(\pm0.6i\) in the base plane?
- Are exactly two sheets of the square-root cover shown?
- Do the sheets exchange across branch cuts rather than behaving as unrelated surfaces?
- Are the real and imaginary branch-cut segments placed as specified?
- Is the surface height based on \(\operatorname{Re}w\)-like structure, with opposite sheets having opposite heights/colors where appropriate?

Monodromy:
- Does a loop around a single branch point indicate sheet switching after one circuit?
- Does the loop around \(z=1\) show a sheet swap?
- Does the loop around \(z=0.6i\) show a sheet swap?
- Does the loop enclosing both real branch points show return to the starting sheet?
- Are monodromy trails visually connected to the surface sheets rather than floating decoratively?

Phase Coloring:
- Does color vary smoothly with \(\arg w\)?
- Are phase discontinuities aligned with branch cuts rather than random seams?
- Are the two sheets distinguishable while still sharing the same phase-color rule?
- Are branch points visually singular or junction-like?

__VISUAL_IMPLEMENTATION__
Surface Rendering:
- Are both sheets smooth and semi-transparent enough to inspect overlaps?
- Are branch-cut seams crisp and readable?
- Are branch points clearly marked and not hidden by the sheets?
- Is the base grid visible beneath the surfaces?

Curve and Trail Rendering:
- Are monodromy loops anti-aliased, arrowed, and spatially aligned with the base plane/sheets?
- Are branch cuts thicker/darker than ordinary grid lines?
- Are real and imaginary axes emphasized without cluttering the figure?
- Are sheet crossings handled without severe z-fighting artifacts?

Composition:
- Does the camera show all four branch points and both cuts at once?
- Is the chosen perspective sufficient to reveal the two-sheeted structure?
- Is transparency balanced so the image is not muddy?
- Is the optional sheet legend or symbolic cue unobtrusive and clear?

__COMPLETENESS_AND_SPECIFICATIONS__
Required Elements:
- Two covering sheets are present.
- Four branch points are marked.
- Real and imaginary branch cuts are drawn.
- Three monodromy trails are present with directional arrows.
- Base \(z\)-plane grid and emphasized axes are present.
- Phase-based color variation is present.

Technical Requirements:
- Output is a single PNG at least 1600×1600 px.
- Static frame only.
- Background is white or light grey.
- The visualization is self-contained and interpretable without external links.

Failure Modes to Penalize:
- Single surface only, with no second sheet.
- Branch points in incorrect locations.
- Decorative cuts that do not align with phase discontinuities or sheet exchange.
- Missing monodromy loops.
- Random colors unrelated to complex phase.
