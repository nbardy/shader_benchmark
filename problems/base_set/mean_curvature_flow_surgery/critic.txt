__MATHEMATICAL_ACCURACY__
Post-Surgery Topology:
- Are there exactly two separated closed surface components after surgery?
- Is there a clear central gap between the two components at the former neck location?
- Are both cut locations capped smoothly rather than left as open holes?
- Do the caps appear near `z = ±0.13` relative to the axis of the dumbbell?

Pre-Surgery Ghost Consistency:
- Is a faint continuous dumbbell ghost surface visible, showing the pre-surgery neck connecting the two bulbs?
- Is the ghost neck very thin compared with the two bulb radii, consistent with a neck-pinch?
- Does the ghost surface align with the post-surgery components rather than floating unrelatedly?
- Are inward flow arrows near the neck present and pointing toward the shrinking/pinching direction?

Curvature and Surgery Encoding:
- Is high curvature concentrated near the new caps and scar rings?
- Are low-curvature outer bulb regions colored blue or another clearly low-curvature color?
- Does the color field vary smoothly over the surface, not randomly?
- Are the two scar rings positioned at the topological surgery cuts?

__VISUAL_IMPLEMENTATION__
Surface Quality:
- Are the post-surgery surfaces smooth and rounded, without faceted polygon artifacts?
- Do the surfaces of revolution look axisymmetric around a common `z` axis?
- Are caps convex and visually continuous with the bulb surfaces?
- Is the ghost surface translucent enough to distinguish it from the main geometry?

Curvature Heatmap:
- Is the blue-to-red curvature map legible across the surface?
- Is high curvature rendered as localized red/orange bands or caps rather than covering everything uniformly?
- Is there a usable colorbar or gradient swatch explaining the curvature scale?
- Are scar rings visually distinct from the curvature colors?

Lighting and Composition:
- Does soft lighting reveal the 3D shape of the bulbs and caps?
- Is the central neck/surgery region prominently visible?
- Is the camera angle suitable for seeing both the axial profile and the separation gap?
- Are there no distracting backgrounds, excessive shadows, or clipping artifacts?

__COMPLETENESS_AND_SPECIFICATIONS__
Required Geometry:
- Two post-surgery components are present.
- One translucent pre-surgery ghost surface is present.
- Two red scar rings are present.
- Curvature-flow arrows near the neck are present.

Technical Requirements:
- Image resolution is at least `1600 × 1600` pixels.
- Static PNG frame, not a sequence or multipanel animation.
- White or nearly white background.
- Anti-aliasing is sufficient for smooth silhouettes.

Benchmark-Specific Failure Modes:
- If the image shows a single unsurgered dumbbell only, mathematical accuracy should be low.
- If the caps are open holes, topological accuracy should be strongly penalized.
- If curvature coloring is random or purely decorative, mathematical accuracy and visual implementation should be penalized.
- If the ghost state is missing, completeness should be penalized.
