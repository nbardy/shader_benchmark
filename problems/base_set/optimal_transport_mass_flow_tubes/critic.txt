__MATHEMATICAL_ACCURACY__
Optimal Map Structure:
- Does the source distribution appear as a filled disk and the target as an annulus with a clear central hole?
- Do flow tubes move mass radially outward according to a monotone disk-to-annulus map?
- Are the trajectories straight-line displacement interpolations from source to target rather than arbitrary curves?
- Does the annulus inner radius appear around `45%` of the outer radius?
- Are there no large-scale crossings inconsistent with radial monotone transport?

Mass Sampling and Interpolation:
- Are many equal-mass trajectories visible, roughly hundreds rather than just a few arrows?
- Are source samples denser in outer rings in a way consistent with uniform area sampling?
- Is the highlighted intermediate slice at `s ≈ 0.55` visible between source and target planes?
- Does the intermediate slice show a partially opened central void but not the full final annulus?

Spacetime Encoding:
- Is vertical position used as interpolation time `s` from `0` to `1`?
- Are the source disk and target annulus placed at opposite time levels?
- Do tube colors or positions consistently progress from blue/source to orange/target?
- Is the map visibly deterministic: one source sample maps to one target sample?

__VISUAL_IMPLEMENTATION__
Flow Tube Quality:
- Are flow tubes smooth, thin, and individually readable?
- Is clutter controlled despite hundreds of trajectories?
- Do tubes have enough lighting or shading to reveal 3D depth?
- Are endpoints anchored cleanly on the disk and annulus distributions?

Distribution Rendering:
- Is the source disk visibly filled, not just an outline?
- Is the target annulus visibly hollow at the center?
- Are mid-slice dots or markers crisp and located on a horizontal plane?
- Are transparency and opacity balanced so the whole transport path is visible?

Color and Composition:
- Is the blue-to-orange time gradient clear and consistent?
- Does the camera reveal both radial expansion and vertical interpolation?
- Is the white background clean and uncluttered?
- Are shadows subtle and not mistaken for additional transport paths?

__COMPLETENESS_AND_SPECIFICATIONS__
Required Elements:
- Blue source disk at `s = 0` is present.
- Orange target annulus at `s = 1` is present.
- At least hundreds of flow tubes or equivalent dense trajectories are visible.
- Intermediate slice at `s = 0.55` is present.
- Time/interpolation axis or visual equivalent is present.

Technical Requirements:
- Image resolution is at least `1600 × 1600` pixels.
- Output is a single static PNG.
- Background is white or nearly white.
- Full source-to-target geometry is visible and not clipped.

Benchmark-Specific Failure Modes:
- If the target is not an annulus, mathematical accuracy should be low.
- If transport paths are random, swirling, or heavily crossing, mathematical accuracy should be penalized.
- If the visualization is only a 2D chart without spacetime flow tubes, completeness should be low.
- If there are too few trajectories to see mass transport, visual implementation should be penalized.
