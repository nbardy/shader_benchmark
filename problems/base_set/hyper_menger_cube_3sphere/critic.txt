Evaluate this shader-generated image based on the 4D Hyper Menger Cube intersection with 3-sphere visualization requirements.


**Inputs**

1. *Spec* – the exact text of Prompt #3 (generation prompt).
2. *Candidate image* – the renderer's output produced from that spec.

**Task**
Provide a *reasoned* score **S** on the integer scale 1 – 10 that measures how well the candidate image fulfils the spec. Use the rubric below (maximum in parentheses, total = 10). Include a short justification (≤ 150 words).

## __MATHEMATICAL_ACCURACY__

**4D Fractal Structure (0-3 pts)**
- Evidence of proper 4D Menger cube construction projected to 3D
- Characteristic fractal cross-sections visible at multiple scales and orientations
- At least 3 iterations of the 4D Menger construction must be visible
- Self-similar structure apparent at multiple scales
- Proper fractal pattern consistency across all visible cross-sections

**Spherical Intersection constraint (0-2 pts)**
- Geometry properly constrained to 3-sphere surface
- Clear spherical boundaries with no spurious geometry beyond unit sphere
- Proper intersection mathematics between 4D fractal and 3-sphere
- No artifacts extending outside spherical domain

**Stereographic Projection accuracy (0-2 pts)**
- Mathematically correct 4D to 3D projection behavior
- Proper handling of projection singularities
- Compression effects near singularities handled correctly
- Topologically consistent projection throughout

## __VISUAL_IMPLEMENTATION__

**Color and transparency mapping (0-2 pts)**
- W-coordinate color mapping: yellow at north pole, orange at equator, purple at south pole
- Semi-transparent material (α≈0.8) properly rendered
- Internal structure visible through transparency
- Smooth color gradients without banding artifacts

**Technical rendering quality (0-1 pts)**
- High resolution (≥1600x1600) image quality
- Multiple light sources creating proper illumination
- Deep space background gradient implemented correctly
- No rendering artifacts or visual glitches

## __COMPLETENESS_AND_SPECIFICATIONS__

**Output format**

```text
Score: S/10
Rationale: <concise explanation>
```

**Scoring conventions**

* A mathematically accurate 4D intersection with proper projection earns *10*.
* Missing or incorrect 4D fractal structure caps the score at *≤ 5*.
* Incorrect spherical constraint or projection errors cap the score at *≤ 6*.
* Wrong color mapping or material properties subtract 1 – 2 points.
* Poor technical execution (low resolution, bad lighting) subtracts 1 point.
