__MATHEMATICAL_ACCURACY__
Dislocation Types:
- Is the screw dislocation represented as a helical line with Burgers vector parallel to its axis?
- Is the edge loop a roughly circular loop near the \(z=0\) slip plane?
- Is the Frank–Read source a bowed segment with two pinned endpoints?
- Is the junction segment present and connected between the loop and helix region?
- Are dislocation segments visually distinct rather than collapsed into one generic curve?

Burgers Vectors and Stress:
- Are Burgers-vector arrows shown next to the relevant segments?
- Are Burgers-vector colors consistent across tubes, arrows, and legend?
- Is the edge-loop Burgers vector parallel to the missing closure of the Burgers circuit?
- Are shear arrows on opposite cube faces consistent with \(\tau_{xy}\)?
- Are slip planes placed at \(z=0\) and approximately \(x+y+z=0.30\)?

Crystal Context:
- Is there a cubic crystal frame with faint lattice points?
- Do planes and lattice serve as spatial context without implying impossible geometry?
- Are pinned endpoints visibly fixed on the bowed segment?
- Does the network read as a dislocation network in a crystal rather than arbitrary colored tubes?

__VISUAL_IMPLEMENTATION__
Tube and Arrow Rendering:
- Are dislocation tubes smooth, glossy, and clearly separated by color?
- Are tangent arrows aligned with the line directions?
- Are Burgers-vector arrows straight and visually distinguishable from tangent arrows?
- Are tube radii appropriate: large enough to see, small enough not to obscure topology?

Planes, Lattice, and Circuit:
- Are slip planes transparent with subtle hatching or grid cues?
- Is the Burgers circuit square readable and close to the edge loop?
- Is the closure-failure vector visible and parallel to the edge Burgers vector?
- Are lattice points faint and not distracting?

Composition:
- Does the camera show all four dislocation elements at once?
- Are shear arrows visible on the cube faces?
- Is the legend, if present, compact and unambiguous?
- Is anti-aliasing adequate on tubes, arrows, lattice points, and cube rods?

__COMPLETENESS_AND_SPECIFICATIONS__
Required Elements:
- Screw helix dislocation is present.
- Edge loop dislocation is present.
- Bowed Frank–Read source with pinned endpoints is present.
- Junction segment is present.
- Burgers-vector arrows and color legend are present.
- Slip planes \(z=0\) and \(x+y+z=0.30\) are present.
- Shear stress arrows are present.
- Burgers circuit with closure failure is present.

Technical Requirements:
- Output is a single PNG at least 1600×1600 px.
- Static frame only.
- Background is white or light grey.
- Main dislocation network is visually dominant over lattice context.

Failure Modes to Penalize:
- Generic crystal lattice with no dislocation network.
- Missing Burgers vectors or Burgers circuit.
- Frank–Read source not pinned or not bowed.
- Opaque planes hiding the dislocations.
- Colors inconsistent between tubes and vector legend.
