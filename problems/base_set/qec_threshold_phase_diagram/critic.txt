__MATHEMATICAL_ACCURACY__
Threshold Scaling Structure:
- Are two distinct decoder panels or surfaces present, one for A and one for B?
- Do curves for different distances cross near a common threshold in each decoder panel?
- Is decoder A's threshold visibly to the right of decoder B's threshold (`0.0105 > 0.0088`)?
- Are threshold planes drawn at the crossing locations, not arbitrarily elsewhere?
- Does the logical error increase as physical error rate `p` increases?

Distance Dependence:
- Are six discrete code-distance tracks shown for `d = 3, 5, 7, 9, 11, 13`?
- Below threshold, do larger distances trend toward lower logical error?
- Above threshold, do larger distances trend toward higher or steeper logical error, consistent with finite-size scaling?
- Are the distance tracks visually ordered rather than randomly tangled?

Probability / Log Scale Representation:
- Is logical error probability represented by height, color, or both?
- Is there a clear crossing ridge around `P_L = 0.5`?
- If a log scale is used, does it avoid impossible negative probabilities and preserve monotonicity?
- Are the plotted quantities consistent with the stated sigmoid model rather than arbitrary curves?

__VISUAL_IMPLEMENTATION__
Curve and Surface Rendering:
- Are the six curves per decoder smooth and legible?
- Are semi-transparent surfaces or ribbons present behind/under the curves?
- Are individual distances distinguishable by position, line weight, or subtle markers?
- Are the threshold planes translucent and not hiding the curves?

Color Encoding:
- Is decoder A consistently blue/cyan and decoder B consistently orange/red?
- Is the logical-error color gradient monotone and readable?
- Are the threshold planes and crossing ridges visually distinct from the data surfaces?
- Does the legend or color coding avoid ambiguity between decoders and error magnitude?

Chart Composition:
- Are axes, ticks, and panel separation clear enough to interpret the diagram?
- Does the 3D perspective avoid excessive occlusion of curves?
- Is the white background clean and non-distracting?
- Are antialiasing and line quality sufficient for small tick marks and curves?

__COMPLETENESS_AND_SPECIFICATIONS__
Required Elements:
- Decoder A and decoder B are both shown.
- Six distance curves are shown for each decoder.
- Threshold plane is present in each decoder panel.
- Crossing ridge or crossing markers are present.
- Legend or equivalent color identification is present.

Technical Requirements:
- Image resolution is at least `1600 × 1600` pixels.
- Output is a single static PNG.
- White or nearly white background.
- The diagram is large enough for panel-level and curve-level inspection.

Benchmark-Specific Failure Modes:
- If the output is a generic heatmap with no threshold crossing structure, mathematical accuracy should be low.
- If thresholds are reversed, with B to the right of A, mathematical accuracy should be penalized.
- If code distance is omitted, completeness should be low.
- If the plot shows arbitrary decorative surfaces with no monotonic error trend, mathematical accuracy should be low.
