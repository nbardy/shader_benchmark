__MATHEMATICAL_ACCURACY__
Boundary Curve Specification:
- Is the main boundary a nontrivial knot-like closed tube, not a simple circle?
- Are ghost trefoil and figure-eight-style boundary curves visible as distinct cyan/magenta references?
- Does the main boundary appear as a midpoint morph between the two ghost boundaries rather than identical to either one?
- Are all boundary curves closed without gaps or broken endpoints?

Minimal / Plateau Surface Behavior:
- Does the surface span the full main boundary curve with no missing regions?
- Does the interior look like a relaxed soap film with saddle structure rather than a flat disk or arbitrary blob?
- Are interior mesh vertices visually smoother and lower-curvature than boundary regions?
- Does the surface avoid obvious self-intersections not implied by the boundary projection?

Curvature Overlay:
- Is low residual mean curvature concentrated over much of the interior?
- Are higher curvature or strain colors localized near boundary bends, necks, or saddle transitions?
- Is the curvature color field coherent over the triangulated surface?
- Does the surface geometry and coloring together communicate approximate area minimization?

__VISUAL_IMPLEMENTATION__
Surface and Mesh Quality:
- Is the soap film semi-translucent and continuous across the entire boundary?
- Are triangulation/grid lines subtle but visible enough to show the mesh structure?
- Is the boundary tube smooth and consistently thick?
- Are ghost curves thin enough not to dominate the main surface?

Knot Readability:
- Are over/under relationships of the boundary visually suggested through depth, lighting, and occlusion?
- Does the camera angle reveal the 3D knot shape rather than flattening it into a messy projection?
- Are the main boundary and ghost boundaries distinguishable by color and opacity?
- Is the main midpoint object centered and large enough to inspect?

Lighting and Color:
- Does lighting reveal the saddle-like shape of the film?
- Is the curvature heatmap readable through the translucent material?
- Are cyan, magenta, dark boundary, and heatmap colors not confused with each other?
- Are aliasing, jagged edges, or transparency sorting artifacts minimal?

__COMPLETENESS_AND_SPECIFICATIONS__
Required Elements:
- Main midpoint minimal-surface approximation is present.
- Main boundary tube `B(u)` is present.
- Both ghost boundaries `T(u)` and `F(u)` are present.
- Curvature coloring and surface mesh/grid are present.

Technical Requirements:
- Image resolution is at least `1600 × 1600` pixels.
- Output is one static PNG frame.
- White or nearly white background.
- Surface occupies most of the frame and is not clipped.

Benchmark-Specific Failure Modes:
- If the output is only a decorative knot with no spanning surface, completeness should be low.
- If the boundary is a circle or simple torus-like loop, mathematical accuracy should be low.
- If the surface ignores the boundary and floats separately, mathematical accuracy should be low.
- If all three boundary states are missing or indistinguishable, completeness and visual implementation should be penalized.
