__MATHEMATICAL_ACCURACY__
Energy Function and Periodicity:
- Does the surface represent the specified periodic two-angle domain \(\phi,\psi\in[-\pi,\pi]\)?
- Are left/right and top/bottom seams treated as periodic rather than as unrelated boundaries?
- Are the three low-energy basins visually located near \((-1.20,0.85)\), \((1.05,-0.75)\), and \((2.35,2.10)\)?
- Is the high ridge or saddle near \((0.05,0.05)\) clearly higher than nearby regions?
- Does the energy surface have multiple wells and ridges rather than a generic random terrain?

Gradient-Descent Trajectories:
- Do the three paths start from the prescribed unfolded positions and descend toward basins?
- Are arrowheads or direction cues consistent with decreasing energy?
- Do paths wrap correctly if they approach a periodic boundary?
- Are trajectories smooth and continuous, with no impossible jumps except through explicitly indicated periodic wrapping?

Landscape Invariants:
- Does the native basin appear as the deepest basin?
- Are misfolded and intermediate basins shallower but still recognisable local minima?
- Are contour lines consistent with the height field, with tighter spacing near steep ridges?
- Is the topology of the landscape basin/ridge structure visually recoverable?

__VISUAL_IMPLEMENTATION__
Surface Rendering:
- Is the height field smooth and adequately sampled, with no visible grid faceting at normal viewing distance?
- Are holes, tears, or clipping artifacts absent from the surface?
- Does lighting reveal the 3D relief without washing out the color map?
- Are contour lines thin, readable, and slightly offset to avoid z-fighting?

Color and Glyph Encoding:
- Does the low-to-high energy color mapping follow a clear monotone progression?
- Are the three trajectories colored distinctly and not confused with contour colors?
- Are the basin glyphs and ridge marker visible from the chosen camera angle?
- Are seam cues subtle but legible, rather than dominating the image?

Composition:
- Is the camera angle oblique enough to reveal height while preserving the \((\phi,\psi)\) layout?
- Are all important elements visible without excessive occlusion?
- Is the flat inset readable and visually connected to the main surface?
- Is anti-aliasing sufficient on contour lines, tubes, glyphs, and surface edges?

__COMPLETENESS_AND_SPECIFICATIONS__
Required Elements:
- Main \(220\times220\)-or-better energy surface is present.
- Twelve or approximately twelve iso-energy contour levels are visible.
- Three gradient-descent trajectories are present and have direction cues.
- Native, misfolded, intermediate, and ridge markers are all present.
- Periodic boundary/seam indicators are included.

Technical Requirements:
- Output is a single static PNG.
- Resolution is at least 1600×1600 px.
- Background is white or very light grey.
- The image is self-contained and does not rely on external data or labels alone.

Failure Modes to Penalize:
- Generic hills without the specified basin positions.
- Flat 2D heatmap only, with no 3D height surface.
- Trajectories that climb uphill or ignore the specified starts.
- Missing periodicity cues.
- Unreadable clutter that prevents basin and trajectory assessment.
