__MATHEMATICAL_ACCURACY__
Coxeter Triangle Angles and Geometries:
- Are there exactly three panels corresponding to spherical, Euclidean, and hyperbolic geometries?
- Does the spherical panel use curved geodesic triangles on a sphere rather than a flat tiling?
- Does the Euclidean panel show a regular reflection tiling with straight line edges?
- Does the hyperbolic panel use a Poincare disk with geodesic arcs orthogonal to the boundary?
- Are the fundamental triangle angle types visually consistent: finite spherical, flat Euclidean, and shrinking hyperbolic cells?

Reflection Tiling Structure:
- Is a central fundamental triangle clearly outlined in all three panels?
- Are cells generated by repeated reflections, producing mirror symmetry across triangle edges?
- Do adjacent cells generally have different word-length colors?
- Does the hyperbolic panel have many smaller cells accumulating toward the disk boundary?
- Does the Euclidean panel extend at least several reflection layers from the central triangle?

Generator and Word-Length Encoding:
- Are the three mirror generators `A`, `B`, and `C` color-coded or labeled on the fundamental triangle?
- Is cell coloring plausibly based on reflection word length or reflection depth?
- Are mirror edges visually thicker or otherwise distinct from ordinary tile boundaries?
- Is the same generator-color convention used across the three panels?

__VISUAL_IMPLEMENTATION__
Panel Quality:
- Are all three panels balanced in size and visual importance?
- Is the sphere shaded smoothly and not flattened into a disk?
- Are Euclidean straight edges crisp and anti-aliased?
- Are hyperbolic circular arcs smooth and boundary-orthogonal in appearance?

Tiling Readability:
- Are tile boundaries sharp enough to inspect the reflection pattern?
- Are cell colors distinct but not garish or visually overwhelming?
- Does the central triangle stand out clearly in each panel?
- Are cells near the hyperbolic boundary small but still legible?

Composition and Legend:
- Is the triptych layout clean, aligned, and non-overlapping?
- Is a mirror-generator legend or equivalent visual key present?
- Is the white background uncluttered?
- Are labels or icons readable if text is used?

__COMPLETENESS_AND_SPECIFICATIONS__
Required Elements:
- Spherical, Euclidean, and hyperbolic panels are all present.
- Each panel has a highlighted fundamental triangle.
- Each fundamental triangle shows three mirror edges.
- Hyperbolic disk boundary is present.
- A generator color legend or equivalent is present.

Technical Requirements:
- Image resolution is at least `1600 × 1600` pixels.
- Output is a single static PNG.
- Background is white or nearly white.
- Tiling lines are anti-aliased and not blurry.

Benchmark-Specific Failure Modes:
- If the image shows only one geometry, completeness should be low.
- If the hyperbolic panel is just a Euclidean tiling clipped to a circle, mathematical accuracy should be penalized.
- If the spherical panel lacks a sphere or geodesic arcs, mathematical accuracy should be penalized.
- If the central fundamental triangles are not identifiable, both mathematical accuracy and completeness should be penalized.
