__MATHEMATICAL_ACCURACY__
Hamming Code Structure:
- Is the code clearly the binary \([7,4,3]\) Hamming code with 7 variables and 3 checks?
- Does the Tanner graph contain exactly 7 variable nodes and 3 check nodes?
- Are Tanner edges consistent with the specified parity-check matrix columns?
- Are all 8 syndromes represented with distinct colors?
- Are the eight coset leaders highlighted, including the zero vector and seven single-bit errors?

Projected Decoding Landscape:
- Does the central graph contain a dense cloud consistent with 128 error-pattern nodes?
- Are edges only between near-neighbour nodes, suggesting single-bit Hamming flips?
- Is vertical placement correlated with Hamming weight and syndrome index rather than arbitrary scatter?
- Are flow arrows generally directed toward lower-score / lower-weight regions?
- Are local ring markers used only for nodes with no lower-score neighbour or visual stopping points?

Codeword and Motif Correctness:
- Are the three specified weight-3 zero-syndrome motifs highlighted distinctly?
- Do highlighted motifs appear as triangular or three-node connected structures, not random red marks?
- Are zero-syndrome/codeword nodes visually distinguished from nonzero-syndrome nodes?
- Is the relationship between syndrome cube, coset leaders, and central landscape visually coherent?

__VISUAL_IMPLEMENTATION__
Graph Readability:
- Are 128 nodes visible enough to suggest the full error-pattern space without becoming a solid blob?
- Are graph edges translucent and thin enough not to overwhelm node colors?
- Are flow arrows legible at the chosen scale?
- Are highlighted leaders and motifs immediately discoverable?

Color Encoding:
- Are the 8 syndrome hues saturated and distinguishable?
- Is the same color mapping used consistently in the central graph, syndrome cube, and legend strip?
- Do red motif highlights remain distinguishable from syndrome colors?
- Are halos/rims used carefully to avoid hiding syndrome information?

Inset Quality:
- Is the Tanner graph layout clean, with variable and check nodes separated?
- Is the syndrome cube clearly a cube or cube-like arrangement with 8 corners?
- Do the insets support the main visualization rather than competing with it?
- Is the overall layout balanced left-to-right?

- Hamming-neighbour edges are present.
- Decoder-flow arrows are present.
- Eight coset leaders are highlighted.

Technical Requirements:

- Background is white or very light grey.

- The image remains readable without relying entirely on text labels.

Failure Modes to Penalize:
