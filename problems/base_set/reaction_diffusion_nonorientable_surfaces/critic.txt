__MATHEMATICAL_ACCURACY__
Klein Bottle Geometry:
- Does the main surface resemble the specified immersed Klein bottle rather than a torus or sphere?
- Are the neck, body, and self-intersection-like visual crossing present?
- Is the parameter seam indicated and located consistently on the surface?
- Does the surface appear nonorientable through the twisted seam cues?

Reaction–Diffusion Semantics:
- Does the pattern look like a Gray–Scott spot/stripe pattern rather than unstructured noise?
- Are seed-derived pattern regions distributed across the surface, not confined to one patch?
- Is the pattern continuous through the visual self-intersection, respecting the parameter surface?
- Does the pattern avoid obvious discontinuity at the seam except for the intended twist?

Twisted Boundary Condition:
- Does the seam show \((u=0,v)\sim(u=2\pi,2\pi-v)\) rather than ordinary untwisted periodicity?
- Are arrows or cues along the seam consistent with reversing \(v\)?
- Does the fundamental-square inset show vertical-edge twisting and horizontal-edge periodicity?
- Is the inset pattern consistent with the pattern on the 3D surface?

__VISUAL_IMPLEMENTATION__
Surface and Pattern Rendering:
- Is the Klein bottle mesh smooth and high-resolution?
- Is the concentration color map crisp enough to reveal spots/stripes?
- Does shading preserve surface shape without obscuring the scalar pattern?
- Are self-intersection regions handled clearly, with no severe clipping artifacts?

Seam and Inset Rendering:
- Is the seam dashed or subtly marked without dominating the pattern?
- Are twist arrows legible and aligned with the seam?
- Is the fundamental-square inset large enough to read?
- Is the color bar or gradient strip clear if included?

Composition:
- Does the camera reveal the nontrivial topology of the Klein bottle?
- Are important pattern regions visible on both body and neck?
- Is the background chosen for strong contrast?
- Is anti-aliasing adequate on seam markers, inset, and mesh silhouette?

__COMPLETENESS_AND_SPECIFICATIONS__
Required Elements:
- Main Klein bottle surface is present.
- Reaction–diffusion scalar pattern is present on the surface.
- Seam indicator is present.
- Twist arrows or equivalent nonorientable identification cues are present.
- Fundamental-square inset with edge-identification arrows is present.
- Color scale or gradient cue for \(B\) concentration is present.

Technical Requirements:
- Output is a single PNG at least 1600×1600 px.
- Static frame near the specified post-pattern time.
- Visualization is self-contained and does not require external data.
- Pattern continuity under the twisted seam is visually assessable.

Failure Modes to Penalize:
- Rendering a torus, Möbius strip, or sphere instead of a Klein bottle.
- Random texture with no reaction–diffusion structure.
- Missing seam/twist cues.
- Pattern discontinuity at the seam with no twisted matching.
- Missing fundamental-domain inset.
