__MATHEMATICAL_ACCURACY__
Braid Word Representation:
- Does the left panel show exactly six crossings corresponding to `σ1 σ2 σ1 σ2^{-1} σ1^{-1} σ2^{-1}`?
- Are crossings ordered vertically from top to bottom according to the word order?
- Are the first three crossings positive `σ1, σ2, σ1` and the last three inverse crossings `σ2^{-1}, σ1^{-1}, σ2^{-1}`?
- Are there three strands/ribbons, not two or four?
- Is the endpoint permutation the identity at the end of the full word?

Reduction Logic:
- Does the middle panel visually indicate the Artin relation `σ1σ2σ1 = σ2σ1σ2`?
- Are adjacent inverse pairs shown cancelling after the relation is applied?
- Does the right panel reduce to three straight parallel strands with no crossings?
- Are the same red/green/blue strands tracked consistently through all stages?

Over/Under Crossing Convention:
- Are positive and negative crossings visually distinguished by correct over/under ordering or signs?
- Are crossing gaps/shadows sufficient to determine which ribbon passes over?
- Are there no impossible intersections where ribbons merge or pass through each other without crossing notation?
- Does the physical ribbon geometry preserve strand continuity through crossings?

__VISUAL_IMPLEMENTATION__
Ribbon Rendering:
- Are strands rendered as finite-width ribbons rather than simple flat lines?
- Are curves smooth and free of jagged corners?
- Does satin/gloss shading reveal ribbon twist and depth?
- Are endpoint dots or anchors present and aligned across panels?

Stage Layout:
- Are the original, reduction, and identity stages clearly separated left-to-right?
- Are reduction arrows or equivalent transition cues present?
- Is the identity braid visually simple and crossing-free?
- Does the middle stage use ghosting/fading or other visual devices to show transformation?

Color and Readability:
- Are strand colors consistent across all panels?
- Are plus/minus markers or crossing labels legible if used?
- Are shadows useful for over/under readability without cluttering the scene?
- Is the white background clean and non-distracting?

__COMPLETENESS_AND_SPECIFICATIONS__
Required Elements:
- Left original six-crossing braid is present.
- Center relation/cancellation stage is present.
- Right identity braid is present.
- Three colored ribbons are used consistently.
- Reduction arrows or equivalent transition indicators are present.

Technical Requirements:
- Image resolution is at least `1600 × 1600` pixels.
- Output is a single static PNG.
- White or nearly white background.
- The full braid in every stage is visible and not clipped.

Benchmark-Specific Failure Modes:
- If the word does not have six crossings, mathematical accuracy should be penalized.
- If the final state is not the identity braid, mathematical accuracy should be low.
- If over/under information is impossible to read, visual implementation and mathematical accuracy should be penalized.
- If the output is just algebraic text with no ribbon geometry, completeness should be low.
