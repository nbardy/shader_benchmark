**Inputs**

1. *Spec* – the full text of Prompt #1.
2. *Candidate image* – the renderer's output.

**Task**
Output an integer score **S** (1 – 10) indicating how closely the candidate image satisfies the spec, and give a concise explanation (≤ 150 words).

**Rubric**

| Aspect                     | Points | Evaluation criteria                                                                                |
| -------------------------- | ------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Tiling topology**        | 0 – 3  | All faces are triangles; exactly 8 meet at every vertex; pattern fills disk without gaps/overlaps. |
| **Geodesic accuracy**      | 0 – 2  | Edges are straight through centre or arcs orthogonal to boundary circle.                           |
| **Checkerboard colouring** | 0 – 2  | Alternating black/white with global two‑colouring; central face black.                             |
| **Boundary & exterior**    | 0 – 1  | Unit circle present, stroke \~4 px, exterior pure white.                                           |
| **Visual clarity**         | 0 – 2  | High resolution, sharp edges, no aliasing or artefacts, conforms to style (no text, no gradients). |

**Output format**

```text
Score: S/10
Rationale: <brief justification>
```

**Scoring notes**

* Any violation of topology (wrong face shapes, wrong vertex valence) caps **Tiling topology** at 0.
* Missing checkerboard alternation or wrong central colour deducts all **Checkerboard colouring** points.
* Minor rendering flaws (thin lines, slight aliasing) subtract 1 pt from **Visual clarity**.
