**Inputs**

1. *Spec* – the generation prompt for Conformal Spiral Mapping.
2. *Candidate* – the PNG produced by the renderer.

**Task** Return an integer **S** (1‑10) plus a ≤ 180‑word rationale.

**Expected visual cues**

* Rectangular grid transformed into spiral pattern.
* Vertical lines become logarithmic spirals.
* Horizontal lines become straight radial rays.
* Preserved orthogonality at all intersections (conformal property).
* 3D elevation based on transformation magnitude.
* Clear color distinction: blue spirals, orange rays.
* Glass-like material with caustic effects.
* Central singularity marker visible.
