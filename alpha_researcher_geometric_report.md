## 10 New Geometric Challenges to Fill Gaps

### 1. **Regular Hexagon Grid** (Beginner)
- **Objective**: Create a honeycomb pattern of regular hexagons
- **Mathematical Context**: Hexagonal tiling, optimal packing
- **Why Needed**: Fundamental tessellation pattern in nature

### 2. **Lissajous Curves** (Beginner)
- **Objective**: Generate parametric Lissajous curves with ratio 3:2
- **Mathematical Context**: Parametric equations, harmonic motion
- **Why Needed**: Classic parametric curve family

### 3. **Cardioid** (Beginner)
- **Objective**: Draw a cardioid using the rolling circle definition
- **Mathematical Context**: Epicycloid with k=1
- **Why Needed**: Important curve in caustics and envelopes

### 4. **Regular Octagon with Inscribed Circles** (Intermediate)
- **Objective**: Construct regular octagon with inscribed and circumscribed circles
- **Mathematical Context**: Regular polygon properties
- **Why Needed**: Geometric construction principles

### 5. **Logarithmic Spiral** (Intermediate)
- **Objective**: Create a logarithmic spiral with golden ratio growth
- **Mathematical Context**: r = ae^(bθ), self-similar spirals
- **Why Needed**: Appears throughout nature (shells, galaxies)

### 6. **Astroid (Four-Cusped Hypocycloid)** (Intermediate)
- **Objective**: Generate an astroid using parametric equations
- **Mathematical Context**: x = a·cos³(t), y = a·sin³(t)
- **Why Needed**: Beautiful cusped curve, envelope of lines

### 7. **Platonic Solid Projection: Dodecahedron** (Intermediate)
- **Objective**: Project a regular dodecahedron onto 2D with visible edges
- **Mathematical Context**: 3D to 2D projection, dual of icosahedron
- **Why Needed**: Most complex Platonic solid

### 8. **Ellipse and Hyperbola Intersection** (Intermediate)
- **Objective**: Visualize conic sections with their intersection points
- **Mathematical Context**: Quadratic curves, algebraic geometry
- **Why Needed**: Fundamental curves in mathematics

### 9. **Islamic Geometric Pattern** (Intermediate)
- **Objective**: Create 8-fold Islamic star-and-polygon pattern
- **Mathematical Context**: Geometric construction, symmetry groups
- **Why Needed**: Rich geometric tradition, algorithmic beauty

### 10. **Superellipse (Lamé Curve)** (Intermediate)
- **Objective**: Generate superellipse with parameter n=2.5
- **Mathematical Context**: |x/a|^n + |y/b|^n = 1
- **Why Needed**: Generalization of ellipse, modern design applications
