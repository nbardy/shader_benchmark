**Objective**
Visualize **abstract group theory operations** as **dynamic kaleidoscopic patterns**, where group elements create symmetry transformations in real-time.

**Mathematical recipe**

1. Focus on the dihedral group D₁₂ (symmetries of a dodecagon)
2. Create base pattern: 12 interlocking spirals emanating from center
3. For each group element g ∈ D₁₂:
   - Rotations r^k: rotate pattern by 2πk/12 radians
   - Reflections s·r^k: reflect across axis at angle πk/12
4. Visualize group multiplication g₁·g₂:
   - Start with identity pattern
   - Apply g₁ transformation (t=0 to t=1)
   - Apply g₂ transformation (t=1 to t=2)
   - Show resulting composition
5. Cayley graph overlay: nodes at vertices of dodecagon, edges show generators

**Styling**

* Kaleidoscope mirrors as metallic boundaries with realistic reflections
* Base pattern: gradient from violet center to gold edges
* Each group operation has unique color signature (hue = 30k° for r^k)
* Smooth morphing between transformations (60 fps animation feel)
* Particle trails show motion paths during transformations
* Cayley graph: glass tubes with internal light, pulsing during operations
* Depth of field effect: sharp center, soft edges
* Dark background with stage lighting
* Resolution 2400 × 2400 px, motion blur for dynamic elements

**Deliverable** Single PNG capturing mid-transformation state with visible Cayley graph
