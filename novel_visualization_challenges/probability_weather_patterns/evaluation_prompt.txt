**Inputs**

1. *Spec* – the generation prompt for Probability Weather Patterns.
2. *Candidate* – the PNG produced by the renderer.

**Task** Return an integer **S** (1‑10) plus a ≤ 180‑word rationale.

**Expected visual cues**

* Normal distributions visible as circular pressure systems with spiral patterns
* Exponential distribution rain with sudden intensity changes
* Cauchy distribution extreme events: tornadoes/lightning at tail positions
* Beta distribution cloud formations with varied density
* Volumetric clouds with realistic light scattering
* Wind flow lines colored by velocity gradient
* Weather fronts where distributions meet
* Ground terrain with weather shadows
* Atmospheric haze and perspective
* Crepuscular rays through clouds

**Scoring rubric**

| Aspect                      | Pts | Explicit checks                                                          |
| --------------------------- | --- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Distribution mapping**    | 0‑3 | Each distribution type has distinct weather signature                     |
| **Atmospheric realism**     | 0‑2 | Volumetric clouds, proper light scattering, realistic haze               |
| **Extreme events**          | 0‑2 | Cauchy tails generate tornadoes/lightning at appropriate positions       |
| **Visual integration**      | 0‑2 | Weather systems interact naturally; fronts and jet streams visible       |
| **Rendering quality**       | 0‑1 | HDR lighting, crepuscular rays, motion blur on clouds                    |

**Output format**

```
Score: S/10
Rationale: …
```
