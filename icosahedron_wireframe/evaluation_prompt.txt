**Inputs**

1. *Spec* – the generation prompt for the Icosahedron Wireframe.
2. *Candidate* – the PNG produced by the renderer.

**Task** Return an integer **S** (1‑10) plus a ≤ 180‑word rationale.

**Expected features**
30 edges, 12 vertices; all edges equal on projection within 3 %. Hidden edges dashed.

**Scoring**
0‑4 topology, 0‑2 equal edge length, 0‑2 hidden‑edge style, 0‑2 clarity.

**Output format**

```
Score: S/10
Rationale: <text>
```
