**Inputs**

1. *Spec* – the generation prompt for Helical Twist Deformation.
2. *Candidate* – the PNG produced by the renderer.

**Task** Return an integer **S** (1‑10) plus a ≤ 180‑word rationale.

**Expected visual cues**

* A cube twisted into a helical shape with continuous, smooth deformation.
* Full 360° rotation from bottom to top, creating DNA-like twist.
* Metallic surface with visible Fresnel reflections at grazing angles.
* Smooth color gradient: blue at bottom transitioning to golden yellow at top.
* Beveled edges maintaining consistent radius throughout the twist.
* Clear three-point lighting with soft shadows.

**Scoring rubric**

| Aspect                    | Pts | Explicit checks                                                              |
| ------------------------- | --- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Twist accuracy**        | 0‑3 | Measure rotation angle at 10 heights; should vary linearly with R² > 0.99.  |
| **Deformation quality**   | 0‑2 | No discontinuities, self-intersections, or distorted edges.                  |
| **Material rendering**    | 0‑2 | Metallic Fresnel visible; reflections physically plausible.                  |
| **Color gradient**        | 0‑2 | Smooth transition blue→yellow; hue variation < ±15° from expected.           |
| **Lighting/shadows**      | 0‑1 | Three distinct light sources visible; soft shadows present.                  |

**Output format**

```
Score: S/10
Rationale: …
```
